# Change Management & Human Gates

The Change Management and Human Gates function as a continuous governance layer that operates across every phase of the Agentic Software Development Lifecycle. It provides governance, oversight, accountability, and controlled decision-making to ensure that AI-generated outputs remain aligned with business objectives, architectural standards, security requirements, compliance obligations, and organizational policies.

This central process governs changes, manages risks, enforces security and compliance controls, and establishes Human checkpoints to ensure that critical decisions and AI-generated outputs receive appropriate human review and approval. It enables organizations to adopt Agentic AI responsibly while maintaining accountability, transparency, quality, and operational control.


## Goal

Ensure that all engineering activities, AI-generated outputs, and software lifecycle changes are governed, secure, compliant, and appropriately reviewed and approved by human stakeholders, while maintaining alignment with business objectives, regulatory obligations, and organizational standards.


## Phase Outcome

A well-governed, secure, auditable, and compliant software delivery process with clear decision records, approved changes, controlled risk management, and traceable human oversight across the entire software development lifecycle.
