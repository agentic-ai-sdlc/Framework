# Phase G: Agent-Driven Operations & Maintenance

The Agent-Driven Operations & Maintenance focuses on the ongoing operation, monitoring, optimization, support, and continuous evolution of deployed software solutions. Autonomous AI agents support human teams by real-time observability, incident triage, root-cause analysis, routine patching, documentation updates, and routine maintenance activities.

Beyond maintaining operational stability, this phase establishes a continuous feedback loop that captures insights from production environments, user behavior, operational metrics, incidents, and business outcomes. These insights are used to improve software solutions, engineering practices, agent prompts, specifications, knowledge repositories, and architecture standards.

Through governed collaboration between human experts and AI agents, organizations can continuously enhance system reliability, operational efficiency, software quality, and business value across the entire solution lifecycle.


## Goal

Ensure that deployed solutions remain reliable, secure, performant, and aligned with evolving business needs while continuously improving software systems, engineering assets, AI capabilities, and operational processes through data-driven feedback and optimization.


## Phase Outcome

A continuously monitored, optimized, and well-governed software solution that delivers sustained business value while supporting ongoing enhancements, maintenance, operational excellence, engineering practices, and organizational knowledge assets.
