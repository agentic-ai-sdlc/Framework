# Foundation Components

Foundation Components establish a shared and reusable information ecosystem that provides AI agents and engineering teams with the knowledge, context, specifications, and architectural assets required to perform software engineering activities effectively and consistently.

**Purpose:** Provide the foundational information, context, specifications, and reusable assets required by AI agents and engineering teams to understand, design, and develop software solutions.

**Outcome:** A trusted knowledge foundation that enables informed decision-making, improves consistency, promotes reuse, and supports specification-driven software engineering throughout the lifecycle.
