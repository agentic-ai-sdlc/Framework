# Phase B: Vibe Design & Prototyping

The Vibe Design & Prototyping transforms high level functional requirements and user expectations into conceptual solution designs and interactive prototypes. The objective is to rapidly explore ideas, validate assumptions, refine user experiences, and establish a shared understanding of the proposed solution before detailed architecture and implementation activities begin.

This phase emphasizes experimentation, visualization, stakeholder collaboration, and early validation to reduce ambiguity, minimize rework, and improve alignment between business and technology teams.


# Goal

Create and validate a conceptual solution vision, user experience, workflows, and prototype that accurately reflects business needs and stakeholder expectations before investing in detailed architecture and implementation.


# Phase Outcome

A validated solution concept, user experience design, workflow models, and prototype, and a detailed Software Requirement Specification (SRS) that establish a shared understanding of the future solution and provide the foundation for specification-driven architecture and design.
