# Agentic AI SDLC Overview

The Agentic AI SDLC is an agent-driven, specification-centric, cyclic model built around central Human Gates. It is designed to integrate AI-native development into every stage of software engineering. It provides a structured approach to leveraging Agentic AI capabilities while maintaining human oversight, security, compliance, traceability, and governance across all phases of the lifecycle.

The model recognizes that successful software initiatives begin with understanding the business context and user needs before progressing through architecture, engineering, testing, deployment, and operations. By combining human expertise with specialized AI agents, organizations can accelerate software delivery, improve quality, enhance productivity, and promote continuous learning throughout the software lifecycle.

The cyclic nature of the Agentic AI SDLC with Human Gates model represents the output from each phase provides input for the next phase.

The Agent-Driven phases exposes the probabilistic nature of the AI development while the Spec-Driven phases enforces the deterministic nature of this development framework. The engineering team need to maintain a balance of the probabilistic and deterministic nature of the phases for optimum output. The Change Management and Human Gates provides governance, oversight, accountability, and a controlled decision-making.


## About the Model

The model consists of three major phases:

### Pre-SDLC Phase

* **Business Vision**
    The Business Vision establishes the strategic foundation for the software initiative by understanding the business context, organizational vision, business objectives, stakeholders, and the value the proposed solution will deliver. This phase ensures that software development is driven by business strategy and desired outcomes rather than technology alone.

### Core-SDLC Phases

* **Phase A. Use-Case Engineering**
    The Use case engineering translates the business vision into well-defined business requirements, use cases, user journeys, functional requirements, and implementation priorities. This phase establishes the functional foundation for the solution and produces the artifacts required for specification-driven engineering within the Agentic AI SDLC Framework.

* **Phase B. Vibe Design & Prototyping**
    The Vibe Design & Prototyping transforms high level functional requirements and user expectations into conceptual solution designs and interactive prototypes. The objective is to rapidly explore ideas, validate assumptions, refine user experiences, and establish a shared understanding of the proposed solution before detailed architecture and implementation activities begin.

* **Phase C. Spec-Driven Architecture**
    The Specification-Driven Architecture converts approved prototypes and Software Requirement Specification (SRS) into detailed technical specifications and architectural designs. Specifications become the primary source of truth for architecture decisions, ensuring consistency, traceability, and alignment between business intent and technical implementation.

* **Phase D. Agent-Driven Software Engineering**
    The Agent-Driven Software Engineering phase leverages specialized AI agents to transform approved specifications and architecture designs into working software solutions. AI agents assist with software development activities such as code generation, code reviews, refactoring, documentation creation, dependency management, standards validation, and engineering automation, while human experts provide oversight, decision-making, and approval at key checkpoints.

* **Phase E. Spec-Driven Testing & Quality Assurance**
    The Specification-Driven Testing & Quality Assurance phase validates that the implemented solution satisfies all business, functional, non-functional, security, and compliance requirements defined in the specifications. Testing activities are derived directly from approved specifications to ensure complete traceability and coverage.

* **Phase F. Spec-Driven Deployment**
    The Specification-Driven Deployment phase promotes controlled, repeatable, and governed software releases using deployment specifications, infrastructure definitions, security policies, and operational requirements. Deployment activities are automated wherever possible through DevSecOps pipelines and Infrastructure as Code (IaC).

* **Phase G. Agent-Driven Operations & Maintenance**
    The Agent-Driven Operations & Maintenance focuses on the ongoing operation, monitoring, optimization, support, and continuous evolution of deployed software solutions. Autonomous AI agents support human teams by real-time observability, incident triage, root-cause analysis, routine patching, documentation updates, and routine maintenance activities.

### Continuous Governance

* **Change Management and Human Gates**
    The Change Management and Human Gates function as a continuous governance layer that operates across every phase of the Agentic Software Development Lifecycle. It provides governance, oversight, accountability, and controlled decision-making to ensure that AI-generated outputs remain aligned with business objectives, architectural standards, security requirements, compliance obligations, and organizational policies.

Together, these areas provide a complete lifecycle from strategic objectives through continuous operations and optimization.
