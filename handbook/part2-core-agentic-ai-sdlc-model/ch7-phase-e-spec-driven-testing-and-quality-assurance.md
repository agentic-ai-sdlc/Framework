# Phase E: Spec-Driven Testing & Quality Assurance

The Specification-Driven Testing & Quality Assurance phase validates that the implemented solution satisfies all business, functional, non-functional, security, and compliance requirements defined in the specifications. Testing activities are derived directly from approved specifications to ensure complete traceability and coverage.

AI agents support automated test generation, test execution, defect analysis, regression testing, quality validation, and reporting while quality engineers oversee testing strategy, acceptance, and governance.


# Goal

Ensure the solution meets defined requirements, quality standards, performance expectations, security controls, and business acceptance criteria before deployment.


# Phase Outcome

A verified and validated solution with measurable quality metrics, approved test results, resolved defects, and documented evidence demonstrating compliance with specifications and acceptance criteria.
