# Foundations & Core Concepts

This section establishes the conceptual foundation of the Agentic AI SDLC Framework. Before diving into a specification-driven and AI-enabled software development lifecycle, it is critical to understand the paradigm shift driving this next-generation framework.


## Introduction to Agentic AI SDLC

The rapid advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AI), particularly Generative and Agentic AI, is fundamentally transforming how software systems are conceived, designed, developed, tested, deployed, and maintained. Traditional Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) methodologies have significantly improved software engineering practices over the past several decades. However, these methodologies were designed for environments where humans performed the majority of engineering and development activities.

Today's software development landscape is markedly different. AI-powered assistants, autonomous coding agents, intelligent testing tools, architectural reasoning systems, and natural language-driven development environments are increasingly collaborating with human teams. Software can now be generated, reviewed, optimized, documented, and validated with varying levels of AI autonomy.

This shift introduces new opportunities alongside new challenges. Organizations must address AI governance, agent orchestration, specification quality, explainability, security, compliance, traceability, and human oversight. As AI becomes an active participant rather than a passive tool, software engineering practices must evolve accordingly.

The Agentic AI SDLC Framework provides a structured, specification-driven model that enables organizations to integrate AI agents into every phase of the software development lifecycle while maintaining architectural integrity, engineering quality, governance, security, and regulatory compliance. The framework emphasizes collaboration between humans and AI agents, ensuring that AI accelerates engineering without compromising accountability or quality.
