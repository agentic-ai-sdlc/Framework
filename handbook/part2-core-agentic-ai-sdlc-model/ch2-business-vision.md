# Business Vision

The Business Vision is an important phase even though it is outside the perview of the core Agentic AI Software development Lifycycle.

Establishes the strategic foundation for the software initiative by understanding the business context, organizational vision, business objectives, stakeholders, and the value the proposed solution will deliver. This phase ensures that software development is driven by business strategy and desired outcomes rather than technology alone.


## Goal

Develop a shared understanding of why the software should be built, who it serves, the business value it delivers, and how it aligns with the organization's strategic objectives.


## Phase Outcome

A shared understanding of the business vision, strategic objectives, stakeholder expectations, and the value the proposed solution will deliver. This phase establishes the business foundation and strategic direction for the software initiative.
