# Quality Assurance Components

Quality Assurance Components establish a governed quality ecosystem that enables continuous validation, traceability, testing, security verification, monitoring, and operational assurance across the software lifecycle.

**Purpose:** Provide the testing, validation, security, monitoring, and traceability assets required to verify software quality and support governed software delivery.

**Outcome:** A trusted quality assurance environment that ensures software solutions meet defined business, functional, technical, security, and compliance requirements while maintaining reliability and operational readiness.
